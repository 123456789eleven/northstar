# Fun brief: high-grade software that is fun to open

Owner (2026-11-10): "Work on the gamification of the app. Every tab needs to work better and be less formal. High-grade software, but also fun. Elite, addictive the way the best sports and streaming apps are. Score, highlights, and most importantly money and friends." Also: the corny gamey feeling is still unwanted.

## The line between fun and corny
Fun = quick, warm, a little cheeky, specific, kinetic, rewarding. Corny = cartoon icons, "Awesome job, champ", confetti, fake urgency, slang, emoji, exclamation marks, XP/level-up/quest/loot words. Keep numbers precise and sober (money is never joked about). Humour only on low-stakes moments and empty states.

## Voice (replaces the "dry and minimal" rule for the surfaces you touch)
- Second person, short sentences, contractions, active verbs. Think a sharp sports commentator who also respects you: "New personal best." "Three days clear of overspending." "Aisha has been waiting 52 days. Bring her back into the game."
- Celebrate real events with the real number and why it matters. Never generic praise.
- Never guilt, never scold, never loss language. A missed day is "Back on the board tomorrow."
- No emoji, no exclamation marks, no slang, no ALL CAPS shouting.

## Engagement principles (honest, not manipulative)
1. Make progress visible: a score or standing on every screen that matters, with its inputs shown.
2. Records and seasons: personal bests, season standing, you against your own past (a "ghost" of your best week/month). Sports framing, no points currency.
3. Highlights: surface real events as short reel cards (net worth record, biggest saving week, longest run, reconnected with a friend). Reels are finite: end with "You're caught up."
4. Anticipation: "Up next" (what is coming, what is closest to a record or milestone).
5. Juice on completion: one subtle moment (aura ring, count-up, gentle sound if enabled, haptic tick), never blocking, never repeated spam.
6. No dark patterns: no fake scarcity, no countdown pressure, no guilt notifications, no infinite scroll, nothing hidden behind engagement.
7. Everything derives from existing data. No schema changes unless a builder's brief says so.

## Rules that stay
Palette: raven black, powder blue (interactive), poppy red only for behind/overdue/loss, gold only for earned records and milestones. Glass is a layer (src/styles/liquid.css). Phone 390 and desktop 1440 equal; tap targets 32px+; AA text; reduced motion/transparency respected; no new dependencies; privacy rules in CLAUDE.md (no advice, no trades, data stays on device).
