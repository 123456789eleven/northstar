N 05RoadmapNo dates, no promises

What has shipped, and what might be next.

Written plainly. Nothing under “Next” is a promise or has a date. Northstar is built by one person for their own use first, so the order of work follows what is useful day to day.

  • ShippedPlanner with week and day views and time-blocking

  • ShippedCareer case-strength meter and People cadence
