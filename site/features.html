Today

Your day on one screen.

  • A strip of due today, overdue, done today and habits left, so you see where the day stands in a second.
  • Suggested focus is chosen by priority and due date; pin the ones you commit to.
  • Next event, quarter goal progress and gentle nudges (for example overdue tasks, no backup yet) sit beside the list.

Planner

Week and day views for events and tasks.

  • Week and day views show events and tasks together, so a task due Friday sits next to Friday's meetings.
  • Create events with a time, location and notes; "Plan my day" surfaces high-priority tasks with no date.
  • Read-only Google Calendar events can appear here once you connect it (opt-in).

Tasks

Capture in one line, work from a queue.

  • One-line capture: call mom fri !1 #career sets the due date, priority and the goal link.
  • Recurring tasks (daily, weekly, monthly) with monthly dates that do not drift in short months.
  • Keyboard work: j/k to move, x to complete, Enter to open.

Goals

Life, year and quarter goals with real progress.

  • Goals live at life, year or quarter horizons and are broken into milestones.
  • Link tasks to a goal; progress follows the work you actually complete.
  • Quiet goals get flagged: no movement in 14 days becomes a Command proposal for a next step.

Habits

Daily check-ins and streaks.

  • Check a habit in with one tap from its page or from Today.
  • Streaks and completion stats show the pattern, not just the last day.
  • Each check-in is written to your History timeline.

Finance

Import, categorize, budget, see net worth.

  • Import CSV or OFX/QFX statements from your bank. Duplicates are detected, and an import can be undone in one click.
  • Overview, transactions, budgets and net worth tabs; merchant rules categorize repeats for you.
  • Quick add from the capture bar: $12.50 chipotle. No bank login is ever asked for; the live bank feed is optional and off by default.

Food

Meals, targets and what is left today.

  • Log meals in slots (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks) or from the capture bar: ate oatmeal 350cal.
  • "Left today" shows calories and protein remaining against your targets.
  • Save meal templates for what you eat on repeat; optional Open Food Facts search is off until you enable it.

Body

Weight trend and where it is heading.

  • Log a weight in one line (w 136.4); the chart shows a 7-day average over faint daily points.
  • Change this week, 4-week rate and projected date to reach your goal weight.
  • Calorie and protein guidance is derived from your weight and training days, and feeds the Food targets.

Learning

Courses, books and study time.

  • Track items as planned, active or completed, with progress per item.
  • A study log records time spent, building a streak.
  • Finished items become milestones on your timeline.

People

Keep up with the people who matter.

  • Set a contact cadence per person and see who is overdue.
  • Log contact and notes; import contacts from a .vcf file you pick.
  • When someone is overdue, Command proposes a check-in task you can approve.

Journal

Write privately, with an immersive focus mode.

  • Daily entries with prompts, templates, #tags, mood and energy scores.
  • Focus mode hides everything but the page so you can just write.
  • Month map, word counts and a 90-day mood chart. The journal never leaves the device unless you turn on encrypted sync.

History

One timeline for your whole life.

  • Every meaningful action (task done, goal reached, course finished, contact made) is logged to one timeline.
  • The Achievements tab collects the milestones.
  • Search your whole history from the palette with Ctrl K; search runs on-device.

Review

A weekly review you can lock.

  • A digest of the week (tasks done, spending change, habits) is filled in for you.
  • Rate the week and write reflections; ratings trend over time.
  • Lock a finished review so it stays as written.

Command

Proposals from your own data. You approve.

  • Rules look at your data and propose actions: reschedule an overdue task, add a next step to a stalled goal, check in with someone, apply a merchant category.
  • Accept, modify, dismiss or snooze each one, with the evidence and logic shown. Nothing changes until you approve.
  • Optional auto-apply for low-risk rules only; it is off by default, logged on the timeline and can be undone for 24 hours.

Across the app

Things that work everywhere.

  • Capture bar. One line creates tasks, transactions, meals, weights, wins and journal entries. Press / to focus it.
  • Search and commands. Ctrl K (Cmd K on Mac) searches everything on-device and runs commands.
  • Data Hub. Import CSV, OFX/QFX statements, .ics calendars and .vcf contacts; export a full JSON backup and restore it in one step.
  • Encrypted sync. Optional, end-to-end encrypted, to a Supabase project you control. See Security.
  • Google Calendar. Optional, read-only. Events are copied into your local database.
  • Installable and offline. A PWA for desktop, Android and iPhone. See Install.

Northstar. A private, local-first life operations app.
