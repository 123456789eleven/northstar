Today

Your day on one screen.

  1. 1Where the day stands. Due, overdue, done and habits left, counted live from your records.
  2. 2Suggested focus. Chosen by priority and due date. Pin the ones you commit to.
  3. 3Context beside the list. Quarter goals, the next event and gentle nudges such as overdue work or no backup yet.

Across the app

Things that work everywhere.

  • Capture bar. One line creates tasks, transactions, meals, weights, wins and journal entries. Try the demo.
  • Search and commands. Searches everything on-device and runs commands from the keyboard.
  • Data Hub. Import CSV, OFX/QFX statements, .ics calendars and .vcf contacts; export a full JSON backup and restore it in one step.
  • Encrypted sync. Optional, end-to-end encrypted, to a Supabase project you control. See Security.
  • Google Calendar. Optional, read-only. Events are copied into your local database.
  • Installable and offline. A PWA for desktop, Android and iPhone. See Install.
