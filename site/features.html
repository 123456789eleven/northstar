Fourteen modules. One data model under all of them.

01

Module 01 of 14

Today

Your day on one screen.

  1. 01.1Where the day stands. Due, overdue, done and habits left, counted live from your records.
  2. 01.2Suggested focus. Chosen by priority and due date. Pin the ones you commit to.
  3. 01.3Context beside the list. Quarter goals, the next event and gentle nudges such as overdue work or no backup yet.
02

Module 02 of 14

Planner

Week and day views for events and tasks.

  1. 02.1Week and day views. Step through weeks; today is always one click away.
  2. 02.2Events and tasks together. A task due Friday sits beside Friday’s meetings.
  3. 02.3Plan my day. Surfaces high-priority tasks that have no date yet.
03

Module 03 of 14

Tasks

Capture in one line, work from a queue.

  1. 03.1The queue at a glance. Open, overdue, due today and done in the last 14 days.
  2. 03.2Keyboard work. j and k move, x completes, Enter opens.
  3. 03.3Details in a side panel. Due date, priority, recurrence and the goal link, without leaving the list.
04

Module 04 of 14

Goals

Life, year and quarter goals with real progress.

  1. 04.1Life, year and quarter. Goals live at three horizons, filtered by status and area.
  2. 04.2Linked work. Progress follows the tasks you actually complete.
  3. 04.3Milestones. Break a goal into steps you can tick off. Quiet goals become a Command proposal.
05

Module 05 of 14

Habits

Daily check-ins and streaks.

  1. 05.1One tap a day. Check in from here or from Today.
  2. 05.2Streaks with history. Edit a habit any time; every check-in is written to your timeline.
06

Module 06 of 14

Finance

Import, categorize, budget, see net worth.

  1. 06.1Six tabs, one ledger. Overview, transactions, budgets, net worth, investments and import.
  2. 06.2The month in four numbers. Income, spending, net and savings rate.
  3. 06.3Charts with a table view. Every chart can switch to an accessible table.
07

Module 07 of 14

Food

Meals, targets and what is left today.

  1. 07.1What is left today. Calories and protein remaining against your targets.
  2. 07.2Meal slots. Add from favorites, copy yesterday or save a template.
  3. 07.3Repeat meals in a tap. Favorites and templates for what you eat often.
08

Module 08 of 14

Body

Weight trend and where it is heading.

  1. 08.1Log in one line. Here, or w 136.4 in the capture bar.
  2. 08.2Rate and projection. Change this week, 4-week rate and the date you reach your goal.
  3. 08.3Trend over noise. A 7-day average line over faint daily weigh-ins.

09

Module 09 of 14

Learning

Courses, books and study time.

  1. 09.1Planned, active, completed. Courses, books, skills and certifications with progress.
  2. 09.2Log study time. One tap adds a session and keeps the streak going.
  3. 09.3A weekly target. Study time measured against a goal you set.
10

Module 10 of 14

People

Keep up with the people who matter.

  1. 10.1Who is due. Each person has a contact cadence; overdue check-ins rise to the top.
  2. 10.2One tap to log contact. Notes and history sit under Details.
  3. 10.3Birthdays ahead. The next 30 days at a glance.
11

Module 11 of 14

Journal

Write privately, with an immersive focus mode.

  1. 11.1Prompts and templates. Start from a prompt or write freely.
  2. 11.2Focus mode. Hides everything but the page.
  3. 11.3Patterns over time. A month map of writing days and a 90-day mood chart.
12

Module 12 of 14

History

One timeline for your whole life.

  1. 12.1Timeline and achievements. Milestones collect on their own wall.
  2. 12.2Filter by area. Career, health, finance, learning, people and more.
  3. 12.3One timeline. Tasks done, goals reached, wins and journal entries, in order.
13

Module 13 of 14

Review

A weekly review you can lock.

  1. 13.1A digest written for you. Tasks done, goals, spending, meals, study and people for the week, plus what slipped.
  2. 13.2Ratings trend. Rate each week and watch the line.
  3. 13.3Lock it. Finished reviews stay exactly as written.
14

Module 14 of 14

Command

Proposals from your own data. You approve.

  1. 14.1What needs you. Open proposals, what needs approval and what was auto-applied.
  2. 14.2Proposals from your data. Overdue tasks, stalled goals, people to check in with, merchant categories.
  3. 14.3You decide. Accept, modify, dismiss or snooze. Evidence and logic are one click away.
§ +Across the app6 entries
+

Things that work on every page.

Capture bar
One line creates tasks, transactions, meals, weights, wins and journal entries. Try the miniature.
Search and commands
Searches everything on-device and runs commands from the keyboard.
Data Hub
Import CSV, OFX/QFX statements, .ics calendars and .vcf contacts; export a full JSON backup and restore it in one step.
Encrypted sync
Optional, end-to-end encrypted, to a Supabase project you control. See Security.
Google Calendar
Optional, read-only. Events are copied into your local database.
Installable and offline
A PWA for desktop, Android and iPhone. See Install.
