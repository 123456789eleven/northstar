N 01FeaturesToday, Plan, Money, Areas, You

Your day, your money and your health, in one place.

The app has five places in its navigation: Today, Plan, Money, Areas and You. Here is what each one does, shown with the built-in demo data. Use the index to jump.

01

Today

Today

Your plan for the day, and where the day stands.

  1. 01.1Today’s plan. Three to five actions picked from your goals and routines. Each one ticks itself off when the matching record appears, such as a weigh-in, a meal or a journal entry, so there is nothing to check by hand.
  2. 01.2Two numbers that matter. Calories against your target and money left this month, as rings with the figures beside them.
  3. 01.3Focus and agenda. A few pinned tasks and the day’s time blocks from the planner.
  4. 01.4A quiet missed day. If nothing was logged, the page says so in one line and moves on.
02

Plan

Plan

Tasks, the planner, goals and habits.

  1. 02.1Tasks. Capture in one line, work from a queue and swipe to complete on a phone. On a keyboard, j and k move and x completes.
  2. 02.2Planner. Week and day views with time-blocking, a now-line and protected blocks.
  3. 02.3Goals and seasons. Life, year and quarter goals with milestones. Progress follows the tasks you finish, and a coach line states the gap, such as how many weigh-ins you are behind pace.
  4. 02.4Habits. Weekly rings and a month heat map. A missed day does not reset anything.
03

Money

Money

What is safe to spend, what is coming, and whether net worth is growing.

  1. 03.1Safe to spend. One figure for what is left this month across your budgeted categories, with the pace shown against the days left.
  2. 03.2Bills and set-asides. Recurring bills with due dates and a monthly set-aside for each, plus subscriptions detected from your transactions.
  3. 03.3Net worth. A path over time with a target line, updated when you update balances.
  4. 03.4Investments. Holdings such as a Roth IRA and an index fund, entered by you, with their value over time.
  5. 03.5Bring data in yourself. CSV and OFX/QFX import with duplicate detection and undo. An optional SimpleFIN live feed is off by default and needs your own setup, see Privacy.
04

Areas

Areas

Body, mind, career and people, each with its real inputs.

  1. 04.1One overview. Each area shows a ring toward a goal you set, with the records behind it listed beside it.
  2. 04.2Inputs, not scores. Every figure links to the entries it was counted from.
  3. 04.3Everything is optional. Use one area or all four. Nothing is required to be filled in.
05

Areas, Body

Body

Weight path and training.

  1. 05.1Log in one line. On the page, or w 136.4 in the capture bar.
  2. 05.2A path, not a number. A 7-day average over faint daily weigh-ins, a pace line toward your goal and a flag every five pounds.
  3. 05.3Rate and projection. Change this week, the four-week rate, what is left to go and the date you reach the goal at this pace.
  4. 05.4Training. A Training tab sits beside Weight.
06

Areas, Body

Food

Calories and protein against your targets.

  1. 06.1What is left today. A calorie ring toward your target with protein beside it, filling as you log.
  2. 06.2Meal slots. Add from favorites, copy yesterday or save a template. A line like ate oatmeal 350cal files into the right slot by time.
  3. 06.3Close the gap. Suggestions show what would close the gap for protein or calories.
  4. 06.4Optional food search. Open Food Facts search is off by default. See Privacy.
07

Areas, Mind

Mind

Writing and learning.

  1. 07.1Journal. Prompts, templates and a focus mode that hides everything but the page. Entries stay on the device.
  2. 07.2Mood chart. A month map of writing days and a 90-day mood chart.
  3. 07.3Learning and focus runs. Courses, books and certifications with progress, and a count-up timer with pause, resume and an optional target.
08

Areas, Career

Career

Turn daily work into evidence.

  1. 08.1Wins in one line. Log what went well and add a number with #impact 3h/week. Wins also land on your timeline.
  2. 08.2Case strength. A meter scores coverage across wins, projects, leadership, feedback and goals from your own records, and names the next thing to strengthen.
  3. 08.3Project, leadership and 1:1 notes. Tabs keep the evidence for each, so a review conversation starts from facts.
09

Areas, People

People

Keep up with the people who matter.

  1. 09.1Cadence. Each person has a contact cadence, overdue check-ins rise to the top and one tap logs a contact.
  2. 09.2Closeness. People are grouped by how close you are, with the time since you last spoke.
  3. 09.3Birthdays ahead. The next thirty days at a glance, from contacts you add or import from a .vcf.
10

You

You

Milestones, the season, a weekly recap, and how Northstar runs on this device.

  1. 10.1Milestones. Things you finished, dated and listed like a logbook. A gold mark means it was earned, and nothing else gets one.
  2. 10.2Weekly recap. The week in numbers, with an image of it drawn on your device that you can save or share. Nothing is uploaded to make it.
  3. 10.3Season. The quarter at a glance: goals, ratings over time and a lock so finished reviews stay as written.
  4. 10.4Intel. Suggestions from your own data, such as an overdue task or an uncategorized merchant. Accept, change, dismiss or snooze, with the evidence one click away.
  5. 10.5Settings and Data. Import, export, and a backup as a plain JSON file or one encrypted with a passphrase.
+

Across the app

Things that work on every page.

  1. +.1Capture bar and orb. One line creates tasks, transactions, meals, weights, wins and journal entries. On a phone, the capture button in the bottom bar opens a sheet for the same line.
  2. +.2Search that opens the record. Ctrl K searches everything on the device, and picking a result opens the exact record. It also runs commands from the keyboard.
  3. +.3Data. Import CSV, OFX/QFX statements, .ics calendars and .vcf contacts. Export a full backup, as plain JSON or encrypted with a passphrase, and restore it in one step.
  4. +.4Encrypted sync. Optional, end-to-end encrypted, to a Supabase project you control. See Security.
  5. +.5Google Calendar. Optional, read-only. Events are copied into your local database.
  6. +.6Installable and offline. A web app for desktop, Android and iPhone. See Install.
Northstar

A calm, private, local-first life dashboard. Free to use. Your records stay on your device.
