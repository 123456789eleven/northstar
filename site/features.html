N 01Features5 hubs

Five hubs. One data model under all of them.

Today, Plan, Skills, Mind and You hold every page; the older pages are tabs inside them. What each one does, shown with the built-in demo dataset. Use the index to jump, or press / to searchscroll.

01

Hub 1 of 5

Today

Your day on one screen, and a mission board that finishes itself.

  1. 01.1Missions that complete themselves. Up to five a day, built from your goals and routines: a weigh-in, a protein target, a lift, a journal line, a win, the person you have not spoken to in longest. Each one is done when the underlying record appears, not when you tick it.
  2. 01.2Focus, agenda and one find. A three-slot focus bar, today’s agenda from the Planner, and a single factual line such as how many days this week you logged protein.
  3. 01.3Where the day stands. Due today, overdue, done and habits left, counted live from your records.
  4. 01.4Level, weekly streak and recent XP sit in a rail on desktop, each with a “Why do I earn XP?” link. Calm mode hides all of it.
02

Hub 2 of 5

Plan

What you will do, when, and why. Tasks, Planner, Goals and Habits are tabs here.

  1. 02.1Overview first. The season boss, this week’s focus, habit check-ins for the week and your next time block, before you open a single tab.
  2. 02.2Tasks. Capture in one line, work from a queue, swipe to complete on a phone. On a keyboard j and k move, x completes.
  3. 02.3Planner time-blocking. Put tasks and events into time slots in week and day views, with a now-line and protected time blocks shown in the Body color.
  4. 02.4Goals as a quest log. Life, year and quarter goals with milestones and progress that follows the tasks you complete. The top quarter goal is the season boss, and its HP is its progress.
  5. 02.5Habits. Weekly rings, a week of check-in dots and a month heat map. A missed day does not reset anything.
03

Hub 3 of 5, Skills

Constellation

Five skills, five stars. Levels come from what you already log.

  1. 03.1One star per skill. Body, Money, Mind, Career and People each have their own level, and the stars brighten and join as you level them. Every star has a table view.
  2. 03.2Experience is derived. It is computed from records you already keep, so there is no extra data entry, and every number has a “Why?” line.
  3. 03.3Weekly streak with rest days. Active days out of a weekly target, with two rest tokens a week. It counts weeks in a row, never a daily chain.
  4. 03.4Optional. Calm mode in Settings turns levels, quests, XP and the moments off and keeps everything else.
04

Skills

Body

Weight path, with a flag every five pounds.

  1. 04.1Log in one line. On the page, or w 136.4 in the capture bar.
  2. 04.2A path, not a number. A 7-day average over faint daily weigh-ins, a healthy-pace line toward your goal and a checkpoint flag every five pounds.
  3. 04.3Rate and projection. Change this week, the four-week rate, what is left to go and the date you reach the goal at this pace.
  4. 04.4Training ring. A Training tab sits beside Weight.

05

Skills, inside Body

Food

Meals, with macro rings that fill through the day.

  1. 05.1What is left today. Calories and protein as rings that fill as you log, against targets you set.
  2. 05.2Meal slots. Add from favorites, copy yesterday or save a template. A line like ate oatmeal 350cal files into the right slot by time.
  3. 05.3Hit your numbers. Suggestions show what would close the gap for protein or calories.
  4. 05.4Optional food search. Open Food Facts search is off by default. See Privacy.
06

Skills

Money

Budget health, savings and a monthly boss.

  1. 06.1Budget health bars. Each budget shows how much is left, and the month’s boss is simply staying on budget.
  2. 06.2The month in four numbers. Income, spending, net and savings rate, plus a net worth path with milestones.
  3. 06.3Bring data in yourself. CSV and OFX/QFX import with duplicate detection and undo. An optional SimpleFIN live feed is off by default.
  4. 06.4Charts with a table view. Every chart can switch to an accessible table.
07

Skills

Career

Turn daily work into evidence.

  1. 07.1Wins in one line. Log what went well and add a number with #impact 3h/week. Wins also land on your timeline.
  2. 07.2Case strength. A promotion-case meter scores coverage from your own records across wins, project, leadership, feedback and goals, and names the next thing to strengthen.
  3. 07.3Project, leadership and 1:1 notes. Tabs keep the evidence for each, so a review conversation starts from facts.
08

Skills

People

Keep up with the people who matter.

  1. 08.1A circle, not a list. People sit in orbits by closeness, and a glow fades gently as time passes since you last spoke.
  2. 08.2Cadence. Each person has a contact cadence, overdue check-ins rise to the top and one tap logs a contact.
  3. 08.3Birthdays ahead. The next thirty days at a glance, from contacts you add or import from a .vcf.
09

Hub 4 of 5

Mind

Writing and learning: what you think, and what you are getting better at.

  1. 09.1Journal. Prompts, templates and a focus mode that hides everything but the page. Entries stay on the device.
  2. 09.2Mood constellation. A month map of writing days and a 90-day mood chart, with a writing ring for the week.
  3. 09.3Learning and focus runs. Courses, books and certifications with progress, and a focus run: a count-up timer with pause, resume and an optional target.
10

Hub 5 of 5

You

Your progress, your season, and how Northstar runs on this device.

  1. 10.1Trophies. Milestones collect on a shelf, with the full timeline below it.
  2. 10.2Season. The quarter’s recap, built from the weekly review: a digest of the week, ratings over time, and a lock so finished reviews stay as written.
  3. 10.3Intel. Proposals from your own data, such as overdue tasks or an uncategorized merchant. Accept, modify, dismiss or snooze, with the evidence one click away.
  4. 10.4Settings and Data. Calm mode, haptics, sound and motion. Import, export, and a backup as a plain JSON file or one encrypted with a passphrase.
§ +Across the app8 entries
+

Things that work on every page.

Capture bar and orb
One line creates tasks, transactions, meals, weights, wins and journal entries. On a phone the capture orb in the bottom bar opens a sheet for the same line, with one-tap quick logs. Try the miniature.
Search that opens the record
Ctrl K searches everything on-device, and picking a result opens the exact record and highlights it. It also runs commands from the keyboard.
Calm mode
Turns the game layer off: levels, quests, XP and the moments. The hubs and layout stay. See the game layer.
Data Hub
Import CSV, OFX/QFX statements, .ics calendars and .vcf contacts; export a full backup, as plain JSON or encrypted with a passphrase, and restore it in one step.
Encrypted sync
Optional, end-to-end encrypted, to a Supabase project you control. See Security.
Google Calendar
Optional, read-only. Events are copied into your local database.
Installable and offline
A PWA for desktop, Android and iPhone. See Install.
