Privacy

Your data stays where you put it.

Northstar is local-first by design, not by policy. There is no backend that could receive your data.

How data stays on your device

The app is a set of static files. Once loaded, it reads and writes a database inside your browser (IndexedDB). It makes no network requests carrying your information. A strict content security policy limits the app and this site to loading resources from their own origin, and fonts are self-hosted.

What is stored

AreaExamples
Tasks and goalsTitles, due dates, progress, recurrence
FinanceTransactions from CSV files you import, budgets
Food and learningMeals you log, courses and lessons
PeopleNames and contact notes you enter
HistoryA timeline of events and milestones
SettingsPreferences on this device

Bank credentials are never asked for or stored. Bank data enters only through a CSV file you pick.

What is not collected

  • No accounts, sign-ins or email addresses.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • No cloud storage or sync.
  • No third-party fonts, CDNs or embeds.

Backups

In Settings you can export a JSON backup of everything, and import one later. The file is created and saved by your browser on your device. Treat it like any personal document: it holds your data in plain form. Importing a backup runs as a single step so a failed import cannot leave things half-written.

Offline and the service worker

A service worker caches the app’s own files so it opens without a connection. It caches application code and assets only, never your data.

Clearing your data

Clearing this site’s data in your browser settings erases your Northstar data from that device. Export a backup first if you want to keep it. Because there is no cloud copy, it cannot be recovered for you.
