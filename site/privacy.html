N 02Privacy4 opt-in connections, all off by default

Your records stay where you put them.

Northstar is local-first by design, not by policy. There is no Northstar server that receives your data. What can leave your device is listed below, switch by switch.

4opt-in connections, all off

§ 01ExplorerFig. 1
01

What leaves your device, switch by switch.

Every connection is off by default. Turn them on here to see what each one would send and to whom. This page changes nothing in the app.

Sent

  • Nothing. With every option off, your records stay in IndexedDB on this device and no request carries them.
  • Encrypted sync: each record as AES-256-GCM ciphertext, plus metadata (account email, table names, opaque ids, counts, timestamps, sizes), to a Supabase project you create.
  • Google Calendar: nothing of yours is sent. Google’s script loads only when you press Connect; events are copied into your local database.
  • Open Food Facts: the text you type into food search, plus your IP address, to world.openfoodfacts.org.
  • SimpleFIN: your setup token once, then your access URL and a date range, through a stateless relay in your own Supabase project. Transactions come back.
§ 02The short version
02

Static files, a database in your browser, nothing else by default.

The app is a set of static files. Once loaded, it reads and writes a database inside your browser (IndexedDB). By default it makes no network requests that carry your records. There are four optional connections, each off until you turn it on.

A strict content security policy limits what the app can connect to, and fonts are self-hosted. This website makes no requests to any other site, sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

§ 04The details
04

What is kept, what is not, and how to erase it.

What is not collected
  • No accounts or sign-ins, unless you turn on sync.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • No cloud storage by default.
  • No third-party requests from this website or the app at rest.
  • No third-party fonts, CDNs or embeds.
What is stored on your device

Tasks and goals, finance transactions and budgets, planner events, habits, journal entries and mood, meals and weights, learning items, people and contact notes, your history timeline, and settings.

Backups are JSON files that you export and import yourself; treat one like any personal document, because it holds your data in readable form.

Offline and the service worker

A service worker caches the app’s own files so it opens without a connection. It caches application code and assets only, never your data.

Clearing your data

Clearing this site’s data in your browser erases your Northstar data from that device. Export a backup first if you want to keep it. Without sync there is no cloud copy that could restore it. With sync on, your other devices and the encrypted cloud copy still have it.
