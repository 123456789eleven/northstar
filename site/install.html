Install

Install Northstar on desktop or phone.

You can use it in a normal browser tab right away. Installing gives it its own window and an icon, and it works offline either way.

Open the app

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the app in Safari. It must be Safari, not another browser.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Northstar from your home screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Open the menu with the three dots.
  3. Tap Install app, or Add to Home screen.
  4. Open Northstar from your home screen or app drawer.

Desktop (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open the app and let it finish loading once.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose Install Northstar (Chrome) or Apps, then Install this site as an app (Edge).
  3. Confirm. Northstar opens in its own window and appears in your apps list.

Offline behavior

  • After the first load, the app’s files are cached and Northstar opens with no connection.
  • Your data is already on the device, so everything works offline: capture, tasks, planner, journal, finance and the rest.
  • Features that need the network, such as encrypted sync, Google Calendar and food search, simply wait until you are back online.
  • When a new version is ready, the app offers to update; your data is not touched.

Data is per device and per browser. Your phone and laptop do not share data unless you turn on sync in Settings. Without sync, use Export backup on one device and Import on the other. On iPhone, a site that is only a browser tab can have its stored data cleared by the system after a long time unused, so add it to the Home Screen and export a backup now and then.

First steps

  1. Open the app and choose Load demo to look around with fictional data, or start empty.
  2. Add a task with one line in the capture bar, for example call mom fri !1.
  3. Create a goal and link a task to it, then plan your day in the Planner.
  4. Export a backup from Settings so you know how it works.
