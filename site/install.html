N 04InstalliPhone / Android / Desktop

Three taps on a phone. One click on a laptop.

You can use Northstar in a normal browser tab right away. Installing gives it its own window and an icon. It works offline either way.

Open the app

3taps on an iPhone

§ 01InstructionsFig. 1–3
01

Pick your device.

This page guessed from your browser; switch if it guessed wrong.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Step 1Open the app in Safari and tap Share.It must be Safari. The Share button is in the toolbar.
  2. Step 2Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.Scroll the share sheet if you do not see it.
  3. Step 3Open Northstar from your Home Screen.It opens in its own window, without the browser bar.

Android

  1. Step 1Open the app in Chrome and open the menu.The three dots at the top right.
  2. Step 2Tap Install app, or Add to Home screen.The wording depends on the Chrome version.
  3. Step 3Open Northstar from your home screen.Or from the app drawer.

Desktop

  1. Step 1Open the app in Chrome or Edge.Let it load once.
  2. Step 2Click the install icon in the address bar.Or choose Install from the browser menu, then confirm.
  3. Step 3Northstar opens in its own window.Pin it to the dock or taskbar like any app.
§ 02Offline behavior
02

After the first load, the network is optional.

What works offline
The app’s files are cached and Northstar opens with no connection. Your data is already on the device, so everything works offline: capture, tasks, planner, journal, finance and the rest.
What waits for a connection
Features that need the network, such as encrypted sync, Google Calendar and food search, wait until you are back online.
Updates
When a new version is ready, the app offers to update; your data is not touched.
Data is per device and per browser
Your phone and laptop do not share data unless you turn on sync in Settings. Without sync, use Export backup on one device and Import on the other. On iPhone, a site that is only a browser tab can have its stored data cleared by the system after a long time unused, so add it to the Home Screen and export a backup now and then.
§ 03First steps
03

Ten minutes to know your way around.

  1. Open the app and choose Load demo to look around with fictional data, or start empty.
  2. Add a task with one line in the capture bar, for example call mom fri !1.
  3. Create a goal and link a task to it, then plan your day in the Planner.
  4. Export a backup from Settings so you know how it works.
