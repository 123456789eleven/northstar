§ 02The game layerFig. 3
02

Your life, levelled. Quietly.

Five life skills are five stars. Every weigh-in, win, journal line, contact and budget day you already log brightens one. Quests complete themselves when the data appears. There is no confetti, no streak to lose and nothing extra to enter.

Level 4Today’s missions340 / 400 XP
  1. Weigh inStep on the scale+15 XP
  2. Write today’s entryOne line is enough+20 XP
  3. Log a winShipped the report+25 XP
  4. Reach out to Aisha22 days since you spoke+12 XP
  5. Review today’s spendingCategorize one transaction+5 XP

Weekly streak: 4 of 5 active days, 2 rest tokens left. Miss a day and nothing resets; weeks in a row is what counts.

Calm mode is on: levels, XP and the constellation are hidden, and missions are plain to-dos. The hubs, the layout and every record stay exactly as they were.

Change a sample number
Optional
Calm mode in Settings turns the whole layer off: levels, quests, XP and the moments. Hubs and layout stay.
Derived, not entered
Experience is computed from records you already keep, such as a weigh-in, a meal, a win or a contact. No new tables, nothing to enter twice, and the same data in the same place if you turn it off.
No nagging
No push notifications and no sound unless you turn it on. Streaks are weekly with rest days, so a missed day never wipes anything.

  1. 07:12

    Step on the scale, type one line.

    weight 136.4 files into Body. The 7-day average and the 4-week rate update; there is no chart to open first.

  2. 08:40

    Work from Today, not from a list of lists.

    Suggested focus is chosen by priority and due date. Tick Study session: election timelines and the linked quarter goal moves with it.

  3. 12:35

    Lunch costs one line.

    $12.50 chipotle becomes an expense in Finance, dated today.

  4. 12:41

    So does what you ate.

    ate burrito bowl 650cal lands in the lunch slot by the time of day, and calories left today drop.

  5. 18:05

    Command notices, you decide.

    A rule sees three uncategorized Chipotle charges and proposes Dining, with the evidence. Accept, modify, dismiss or snooze. Applied changes can be undone for 24 hours.

  6. 21:30

    Write it down before bed.

    journal: long walk, felt clear appends to today’s entry, which stays on the device.

  7. Sunday

    The week writes its own review.

    The weekly review builds a digest from History: tasks done, goals, spending, meals, study and people, plus what slipped. Rate the week and lock it.

§ 04One timelineFig. 5
04

The modules write to one History. Three views read it back.

Because every meaningful action is logged once, in order, the History page, the trophy shelf and the weekly review all see across your whole life instead of one app at a time.
