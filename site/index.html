§ 02Where your data livesPrivacy
02

On your device. Sync is opt-in.

The app is a set of static files. Once loaded, it reads and writes a database inside your browser. By default no request carries your records anywhere.

  1. 02.1Local first. The copy on your device is the source of truth, and the app works offline after the first load.
  2. 02.2Encrypted sync, if you want it. Each record is encrypted on your device before it leaves, to a Supabase project you create. The server cannot read it. How it works.
  3. 02.3Banks, your way. Import a CSV or OFX file you pick yourself. Northstar never asks for bank credentials. A live feed through SimpleFIN is optional and needs your own setup. Every connection.
  4. 02.4Backups are yours. Export everything as JSON, plain or encrypted with a passphrase. Clearing browser data erases the local copy, so export first.
