What it is

An app that works like your own operations desk.

Most tools handle one slice of life. Northstar keeps tasks, plans, money, meals, weight, study, relationships and writing as linked records, so the weekly review, the timeline and the suggestions can see across all of them. It installs on your phone and laptop, works offline, and is free to use.

Private by default

Your data is stored in your browser on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no ads. Anything that leaves the device is opt-in and listed on the privacy page.

One timeline for your whole life

Tasks done, goals reached, courses finished and people contacted all land in one History, searchable with Ctrl+K.

Command proposals you approve

Northstar notices overdue work, stalled goals and neglected relationships, and proposes a fix. You accept, change or dismiss it.

One line is enough

Capture without friction.

The common action should take one line or two taps. The capture bar understands plain text:

  • call mom fri !1 #career becomes a task due Friday, high priority, linked to a goal.
  • $12.50 chipotle becomes a transaction.
  • ate oatmeal 350cal logs a meal.
  • w 136.4 logs a weight.
  • journal: long walk, felt clear adds to today's entry.

Your data, your call

Local first, sync only if you want it.

Northstar works fully offline with no account. If you want the same data on a phone and a laptop, you can turn on end-to-end encrypted sync: your records are encrypted on your device before they leave it, and the server only ever holds ciphertext.
